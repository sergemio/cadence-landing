Privacy Policy

Short version: your timers are stored on your device and the app works offline. Nothing is sent without your consent: usage statistics are sent only if you accept them, and you can change your mind at any time.

No account. No ads. Nothing sent without your consent. Your timers, durations, ringtones, announcements and settings are stored in the memory of your tablet or phone.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your board: dish names, durations, steps, ringtones, spoken phrases, language and volume.
  • A flight recorder: what happens on the device (alarms, failures, app shutdowns), to diagnose a problem. It contains your dish names, your announcements and the device model.
  • Your choices about usage statistics and, if you give it, your type of venue.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of this from the device. Like any Android app, its settings may be included in your device's backup to your own Google account; we have no access to it.

What can leave your device, and only when you decide

  • Sending the log (button in Settings) opens your device's share menu. The file goes wherever you send it, and we only receive it if you send it to us.
  • Exporting your timers creates a file you share the same way, to restore your board on another tablet.
  • Importing only reads the file you pick in the system file picker.

Usage statistics

On first launch, the app asks whether you agree to send us usage statistics. Declining is as easy as accepting, and you can change your mind at any time in Settings. Until you accept, nothing is sent. Declining erases anything waiting to be sent and stops sending.

If you accept (from version 0.4.29 of the app), we receive:

  • Your usage: the names of your dishes and their steps, their durations and ringtones, when your timers are started, paused, stopped and adjusted, alarms and how accurate they were, the language and volume you chose.
  • Failures and crashes of the app, so we can fix them.
  • The device: model, Android version, language, approximate country, and an installation identifier created by the app that does not say who you are. If you give it, your type of venue.

We never receive your announcement phrases, your name, your email or your precise location. No advertising identifier is used.

These statistics go through Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics), which processes them on our behalf, on servers that may be located outside the European Union, under the contractual safeguards provided by Google. They are used only to improve the app and fix its bugs: never for advertising, and they are never sold or shared. Detailed statistics are kept for 14 months at most, crash reports for 90 days.

Text-to-speech

Spoken announcements are produced by your operating system's text-to-speech (Android or iOS). We neither receive nor record any audio.

Children

The app is made for kitchens, professional or not. It is not specifically directed at children and collects no data about them.

This website

This site is hosted by Cloudflare, which keeps standard technical access logs. Fonts are served from the site itself.

  • Audience measurement. We measure site traffic with Google Analytics: pages viewed, time spent, country, type of device, the site you came from, button clicks and form sign-ups. Google Analytics sets measurement cookies (_ga, kept for up to 13 months) and processes this data on our behalf, on servers that may be outside the European Union. We have turned off Google signals and ad personalization: this data is only used to understand site traffic, never for advertising. You can delete or block these cookies in your browser settings. We also count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies, and clicks on the “Download” buttons (which button, page language, country), with nothing that identifies you.
  • Early access form. If you fill it in, we keep your email, your device type, whether you want to test the Android version, the language of the page and the country given by Cloudflare. Your IP address is not stored. We use your email only to tell you when the app launches and to invite you to the test, never for anything else, and we never sell or share it. The data is stored by Cloudflare and kept until the app launches, then for 12 months at most. To have it erased, simply write to us.

Your rights

We hold only what you chose to send us: usage statistics if you accepted them, a log, an email or the early access form. To access it or have it erased, write to sergemenassa@gmail.com. You may also contact your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).
