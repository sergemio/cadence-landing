Privacy Policy

Short version: your timers stay on your device. In the current version of the app, nothing is sent to anyone unless you share it yourself.

No account. No ads. Nothing sent automatically. Your timers, durations, ringtones, announcements and settings are stored in the memory of your tablet or phone.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your board: dish names, durations, steps, ringtones, spoken phrases, language and volume.
  • A flight recorder: what happens on the device (alarms, failures, app shutdowns), to diagnose a problem. It contains your dish names, your announcements and the device model.
  • Your choices about usage statistics and, if you give it, your type of venue.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of this from the device. Like any Android app, its settings may be included in your device's backup to your own Google account; we have no access to it.

What can leave your device, and only when you decide

  • Sending the log (button in Settings) opens your device's share menu. The file goes wherever you send it, and we only receive it if you send it to us.
  • Exporting your timers creates a file you share the same way, to restore your board on another tablet.
  • Importing only reads the file you pick in the system file picker.

Usage statistics

On first launch, the app asks whether you agree to send us usage statistics (alarms, failures, device model, and the names of your timers and steps). You can decline, and change your mind at any time in Settings. Declining erases anything waiting to be sent.

In the current version, even if you agree, no statistics are sent: they stay on the device. Before the app starts sending any, this page will be updated to say exactly what, to which service and for how long, and you will be asked again if needed.

Text-to-speech

Spoken announcements are produced by your operating system's text-to-speech (Android or iOS). We neither receive nor record any audio.

Children

The app is made for kitchens, professional or not. It is not specifically directed at children and collects no data about them.

This website

This site sets no cookies. It is hosted by Cloudflare, which keeps standard technical access logs. Fonts are served from the site itself: nothing is loaded from Google.

  • Audience measurement. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics: pages viewed, country, type of device and the site you came from. It uses no cookies, does not follow you from one site to another, and identifies no one. We also count clicks on the “Download” buttons (which button, page language, country), with nothing that identifies you.
  • Early access form. If you fill it in, we keep your email, your device type, whether you want to test the Android version, the language of the page and the country given by Cloudflare. Your IP address is not stored. We use your email only to tell you when the app launches and to invite you to the test, never for anything else, and we never sell or share it. The data is stored by Cloudflare and kept until the app launches, then for 12 months at most. To have it erased, simply write to us.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, except what you choose to send us (a log, an email, the early access form). To access it or have it erased, write to sergemenassa@gmail.com. You may also contact your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).
