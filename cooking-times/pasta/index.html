How long to boil pasta

Pasta shapes and how long they take

The thicker the pasta, the longer it cooks: long thin strands are quick, thick tubes like rigatoni take the longest.

  • Spaghetti 9 min
  • Linguine 10 min
  • Penne 11 min
  • Fusilli 11 min
  • Bow ties 11 min
  • Rigatoni 13 min
  • Macaroni 8 min
  • Tagliatelle 7 min

How to boil pasta, step by step

  1. Plenty of water: 4 to 6 quarts of water per pound of pasta (1 litre per 100 g), in a large pot.
  2. Salt at the boil: about 2 tablespoons of salt per gallon of water (10 g per litre), once it reaches a rolling boil.
  3. Drop it in and stir during the first minute: that is what keeps pasta from sticking. No oil needed.
  4. Start the timer when the water comes back to a boil, and cook with the lid off.
  5. Taste one minute early: the pasta should still have a little bite.
  6. Save a ladle of pasta water before draining: its starch binds the sauce.

In a restaurant: par-cook for the rush

Many kitchens par-cook pasta for about two thirds of the time, shock it in ice water, then toss it with a little oil for prep. During service, it only needs one or two minutes in boiling water to finish.

Pasta cooking questions

How long do you boil spaghetti?

Dried spaghetti takes 8 to 10 minutes in salted boiling water, depending on its thickness. Taste it one minute before the time on the package for al dente pasta.

How long do you boil penne?

Dried penne takes 10 to 12 minutes. Mini penne cooks faster, in about 8 minutes.

How do you know when pasta is done?

Bite or cut a piece one minute before the end. Al dente pasta is firm but not crunchy, with only a tiny speck of white left in the center. A clear white line or dot means it needs another minute.

How much salt do you put in pasta water?

About 2 tablespoons of salt per gallon of water, or 10 g per litre. Add it once the water boils, just before the pasta.

Should you add oil to pasta water?

No. The oil floats on the surface and does not stop pasta from sticking. What stops it is plenty of water and a few stirs during the first minute.

How long do you cook fresh pasta?

Thin store-bought fresh pasta (tagliatelle, ravioli) cooks in 2 to 4 minutes and is ready shortly after it floats. Thicker fresh pasta, often homemade, takes longer: follow the package or taste it.

When do you start timing pasta?

Start the timer when the water comes back to a boil after adding the pasta, not the moment you drop it in.
